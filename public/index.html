About Brigade Belvedere

In Love with Bella Vistas

Brigade Belvedere brings Italian-inspired luxury to Bengaluru's thriving Budigere Cross. Spread across 10.75 acres, this prestigious development features G+43 storey residential towers designed for those who appreciate the finer things in life.

With a commitment to sustainability and a vision of Net Zero Emission by 2045, Brigade Belvedere combines luxury with environmental responsibility. The project includes a sprawling 25,000 sq. ft. clubhouse and over 40 world-class amenities.

About Brigade Group

Brigade Group is a leading real estate developer based in Bengaluru, established in 1986. Known for its quality construction and innovative designs, the company has delivered numerous residential, commercial, retail, and hospitality projects across India. Brigade Group focuses on creating modern living spaces with world-class amenities and sustainable practices. With a strong commitment to customer satisfaction, transparency, and timely delivery, it has earned a trusted reputation in the real estate industry. Landmark developments like Orion Mall highlight its excellence in building vibrant communities and lifestyle destinations.
