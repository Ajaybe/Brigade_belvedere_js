About Brigade Belvedere

In Love with Bella Vistas

Brigade Belvedere brings Italian-inspired luxury to Bengaluru's thriving Budigere Cross. Spread across 10.75 acres, this prestigious development features G+43 storey residential towers designed for those who appreciate the finer things in life.

With a commitment to sustainability and a vision of Net Zero Emission by 2045, Brigade Belvedere combines luxury with environmental responsibility. The project includes a sprawling 25,000 sq. ft. clubhouse and over 40 world-class amenities.
